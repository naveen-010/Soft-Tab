    Set up Ctrl W first

    Until you unbind Firefox’s shortcut, Ctrl W can close your tab normally. Soft-Tab does not check your shortcut setup.

    1. Open about:keyboard, find Close Tab, and clear or change Ctrl W.
    2. Open about:addons → gear menu → Manage Extension Shortcuts. Under Soft-Tab, assign Ctrl W to “Soft close the current tab” if needed.
    3. Use Alt Shift W to undo, or click a tab above. You can change the undo shortcut there too.

    On Mac, use Cmd W. In Zen, unbind Close Tab under Settings → Keyboard Shortcuts.

    Hidden tabs stay live and skip Ctrl Tab. Pinned and actively sharing tabs stay open. The × button and closing the window still close tabs normally.

    No page contents are read or saved. A browser restart cannot preserve a live session.
